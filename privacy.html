Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version

D-word does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking and no ads.

What is stored, and where

Your learning progress, favorites, streak, daily count and settings (dark mode, direction, word list) are saved only in your own browser using Local Storage. This data never leaves your device and the site owner cannot see it.

You can delete it at any time with Stats > Reset progress, or by clearing your browser data for this site.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typeface. Your browser requests the font from Google's servers, which can see your IP address.
  • Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in speech function. Depending on your device, the browser may use an online voice provided by your operating system.

Cookies

D-word does not set cookies.

Contact
